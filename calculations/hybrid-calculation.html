| What Is Hybrid Calculation?

Hybrid is Tropical Planets, Gates and Lines with Sidereal Signs. The Human Design mechanics - the Bodygraph, the Type, the Authority, the Definition, the Gates, the Lines, the Profile, the Incarnation Cross - are identical to what a Tropical calculation gives you. The only thing that changes is the Sign label on each planet.

So a Sun that sits in Aries on your Tropical chart may show as Pisces on your Hybrid chart. Same Gate. Same Line. Same Bodygraph. Just a different Sign name - the one Sidereal astrology assigns to that piece of sky.

Tropical (unchanged)

Planets, Gates, Lines

Every Human Design element - Planet positions, Gates, Lines, Channels, Centers, Type, Authority, Profile, Incarnation Cross - stays exactly as specified in the Tropical framework.

Sidereal (swapped in)

Signs

The Sign shown next to each planet is the Sidereal one - the constellation the planet actually sits in, offset from the Tropical Sign by roughly 24 degrees.

Everyday Example

Think of your Human Design chart as a house. Tropical is the house itself - the walls, the rooms, the wiring. That does not change. Sidereal is the street sign outside. Hybrid keeps the house exactly as it is, but hangs the Sidereal street sign at the door - so you can find it by the address you already know.

| Why Hybrid?

People who work with Sidereal astrology often feel their Sun really sits in a different Sign than the Tropical zodiac says - usually the Sign before. That resonance is real, and it matters. But switching a Human Design chart to full Sidereal is a much bigger trade than most people want to make: Planets, Gates and Lines all shift roughly 24 degrees, and your Type, Definition, Profile and Bodygraph can change entirely.

Hybrid separates the two questions. Human Design uses Tropical, and we honour that - Planets, Gates and Lines stay Tropical. But we let the Signs float to Sidereal, so people who read their sky Sidereally can keep doing that without giving up the Bodygraph they know.
